## Intent

An agent should spend its context on engineering decisions rather than rediscovering repository, process, authority, collaboration, and evidence state through disconnected tools.

## Outcomes

- One stable semantic intent vocabulary works through the standalone binary and Harness.
- Humans and agents observe the same event-derived session projection.
- Implementations remain replaceable without changing model-visible intent contracts.
